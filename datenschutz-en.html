2. Principle: no data collection

Drawmory does not process personal data on the provider's servers. The app works completely offline. There are no user accounts, no registration, no analytics or tracking services, no advertising and no disclosure of data to third parties.

3. Data stored on the device

The following information is stored only in the device's local storage:

  • your best results in the levels
  • the templates you drew or imported
  • the names entered in multiplayer mode, so they are pre-filled next time
  • your settings for language, appearance, sounds and game rules

Your drawings themselves are not stored. They are only used to score the current round and are discarded afterwards.

The data is kept in the protected app directory using the browser technology localStorage. It is never transmitted and is removed completely when the app is uninstalled. Within the app, templates can be deleted individually and level results removed under “Settings → Reset progress”.

4. Names in multiplayer mode

If you enter names of students in multiplayer mode, you process personal data under your own responsibility. The names do not leave the device. We still recommend using first names or nicknames only. Without input, the app plays with “Player 1”, “Player 2” and so on.

5. Sharing templates by link and QR code

A template can be passed on as a link or QR code. The link contains the template's name and its lines as a code. This code follows the “#” character and is technically not sent to the web server when the link is opened; it is only read on the receiving device. The QR code is generated on your device. Where you pass the link or image on via your device's share function is entirely up to you.

6. Web version and hosting

Anyone opening a shared link without the app installed reaches the web version of Drawmory. It is provided via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When the page is loaded, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data such as the IP address in order to deliver the page and secure the service. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; the legitimate interest lies in reliably providing the web version. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Details: GitHub privacy statement. After the first load, the web version also works offline; content, results and templates are not sent to GitHub.

7. Permissions

The app does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts or location. To scan a QR code you use your device's camera app, not Drawmory. When you share a QR code image, the file is placed in a temporary app directory and passed on via your device's share function. If you choose “Save image” there, your device asks for permission to add an image to the photo library; existing photos are not read.

8. Internet connections

The app does not open any network connections by itself. It contains no external libraries, no fonts from third-party servers and no embedded content.

9. Distribution through an app store

When you download the app from an app store, its operator processes data independently (such as account, device and time of download). The provider of this app has no influence on this. The operator's privacy policy applies.

10. Purchasing the full version

The full version is a one-time in-app purchase handled through Apple's App Store. Payment and account data are processed exclusively by Apple; the provider does not receive them. Through Apple's interface (StoreKit) the app only learns whether the purchase exists and remembers this in local storage. Apple's privacy policy applies.

11. Opening shared links in the app

The app can receive links of the form drawmory://import#… to add a shared template. The link contains only the template's name and lines. Nothing is added until you confirm.

12. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection under Art. 15 to 21 GDPR, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since the provider does not store any personal data, requests for access or erasure addressed to the provider will generally have nothing to act on. You can delete all data yourself in the app at any time.

13. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated as soon as app features relevant to data protection change. The current version is always available in the app.

14. Contact

If you have questions about data protection, please contact smaehlmann.appdev@gmail.com.
